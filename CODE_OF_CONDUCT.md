# Code of conduct

Art Frames and its layouts follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion on the work. Do not harass, insult or share anyone's private information.

## Report a problem

- To report a problem in private, open the **Security** tab of the [Art Frames repository](https://github.com/brianmuse/art-frames/security), select **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainer can read it.
- For anything that can be public, open an issue.

The maintainer reviews every report and decides what to do. The maintainer can remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
